MASTER PILOT AGREEMENT

This Master Pilot Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on this 24th day of October, 2024, by and between:

MAHARASHTRA STATE INNOVATION SOCIETY (MSInS), a nodal government agency... (hereinafter referred to as the "Authority")

AND

TECHNO-INNOVATE SOLUTIONS PVT LTD, a company incorporated under... (hereinafter referred to as the "Startup")

1. SCOPE OF PILOT

The Startup agrees to deploy its AI-driven analytics solution within the selected municipal corporation for a period of six (6) months. The pilot will encompass data integration, real-time monitoring, and weekly reporting as outlined in Annexure A.

edit Fix

2. TERMINATION

This agreement may be terminated by either party with a 30-day notice. Upon termination, all hardware provided by the Startup shall remain at the deployment site.

3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

All pre-existing intellectual property shall remain with the respective owners. Any new intellectual property developed during the pilot shall be jointly owned.

4. DATA PROTECTION AND CONFIDENTIALITY

The Startup agrees to comply fully with the provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. All data processed during the pilot shall be encrypted at rest and in transit. The Startup shall not transfer any Authority data outside the territorial jurisdiction of India.

5. GFR COMPLIANCE

This procurement is executed under the relaxed norms for Startups as per Rule 173(i) of the General Financial Rules (GFR) 2017, acknowledging the waiver of prior turnover and prior experience criteria.
