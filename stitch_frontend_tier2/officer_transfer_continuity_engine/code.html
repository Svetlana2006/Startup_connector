1. Strategic Context & Objectives

The "Smart Traffic AI Synchronization" pilot was initiated to address critical congestion bottlenecks along the Pune IT corridor (Hinjewadi-Wakad link). Traditional static timing algorithms were resulting in an average of 42 minutes of unproductive transit time during peak hours.

Primary Objective: Implement computer-vision-based dynamic light synchronization to reduce peak-hour congestion by a target metric of 25% within a 6-month evaluation window. The solution leverages existing CCTV infrastructure, passing edge-computed data to a centralized traffic engine.
